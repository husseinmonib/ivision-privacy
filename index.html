Privacy Policy – I-VISION PRO

Effective Date: May 2025

I-VISION PRO is a custom assistant designed for architectural sketch rendering and visualization. This assistant is hosted by OpenAI and operates within their secure infrastructure.

Information Collection

I-VISION PRO does not collect or store personal information. Uploaded sketches, prompts, and other inputs are processed in real-time and are not retained, logged, or shared.

All inputs are used only for generating the requested visual output. No user data is used to train models or for any analytics purposes.

Third Parties

No third-party tools, trackers, or analytics are embedded or used. I-VISION PRO uses only OpenAI’s backend and your local system or browser.

Your Control

You are fully in control of what you upload and input. If you wish to avoid any processing of sensitive content, refrain from submitting it.
